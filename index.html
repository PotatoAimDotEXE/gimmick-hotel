WELCOME TO THE

ESCAPE ROOM

A reading adventure
Single player Party

Enter = play · Esc = close

How to play

  1. Sign the guestbook with your name.
  2. Press Play to enter the hallway.
  3. Pick a door. Each room trains a reading skill:
    • Locate – find key information fast.
    • Understand – apply what you read.
    • Evaluate – judge what is true and useful.
  4. Escape before the timer runs out and beat your best score.

Keyboard: Esc goes back a menu.
