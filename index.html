How to play

  1. Sign the guestbook with your name.
  2. Press Play to enter the hallway.
  3. Pick a door. Each room trains a reading skill:
    • Effective Detective – read the case and catch the culprit.
    • Interrogation Station – find the lie in what you hear.
    • Paranormal Paralegal – read the case file and write a summary.
  4. Each room has one case a day. Come back tomorrow for a new one.

Keyboard: Enter = play · Esc = back.
